Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

How is my bank data kept private?

ApexFinance operates 100% client-side in your web browser. Your bank CSV files are parsed locally using PapaParse and stored in browser localStorage. No data is sent to any server.

What CSV bank formats are supported?

Any CSV file with transaction dates, descriptions, and amounts. Our auto-detection mapper identifies headers automatically, and you can map custom column names with a live preview.

Can I manually add or edit transactions?

Yes! You can manually record one-off income or expenses, edit transaction categories, or flip transaction types between Income and Expense with a single click.

Does it work fully offline?

Yes. Once loaded, ApexFinance runs entirely offline in your browser without requiring an internet connection.
